Create makes a thought. Derive transforms it by the rules. Play lets you defend or challenge it.

Pan · zoom

Copy · move
Double-click · drag within the same area.
Undo
⌘ or Ctrl + Z.

Try Discoveries for a guided beginning. Alpha uses letters and cuts; Beta adds continuous identity lines; Gamma adds modal openings. Unfold · 3D explores the connected surfaces.

The game’s ? explains the active system. Beta and Gamma use Alt/Option for separate copies and Ctrl/⌘-drag for scissors.
