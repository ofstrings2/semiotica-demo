A playground for thought

Semiotica explores C. S. Peirce’s existential graphs through visual composition, checked transformations, semantic games, and connected surfaces.

Ideas and sources

  • C. S. Peirce, Collected Papers 4.512–518.
  • Don D. Roberts, The Existential Graphs of Charles S. Peirce.
  • Ahti-Veikko Pietarinen, Peirce’s game-theoretic ideas in logic.
  • John Baez and Mike Stay, Physics, Topology, Logic and Computation: A Rosetta Stone.
  • Osman Bineev, Laws of Form Alive.
  • Gerald Brady and Todd Trimble, A String Diagram Calculus for Predicate Logic and C. S. Peirce’s System Beta.
  • Fernando Zalamea, Towards a Complex Variable Interpretation of Peirce’s Existential Graphs; Toyo Ito’s Sendai Mediatheque.

This experimental demo includes Alpha, Beta, and an initial Gamma interpretation. The 3D proof engine currently uses classical constant-domain first-order modal T with rigid identity.
